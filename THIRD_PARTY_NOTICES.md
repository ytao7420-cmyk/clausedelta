## Reused runtime and test utilities

The resumable Studionet transaction journal, receipt lifecycle helpers, read scheduler and related tests were adapted from the author's SchemaWitness project under MIT. Its copyright notice is retained in `LICENSE`. The contribution's new work is the paired-specification contract, requirement-state/entailment model, complete-index absence witness, consumer-intent receipt protocol and W3C adapter/tests. These runtime utilities are not presented as a new contribution by themselves.
